I need to identify high-amplitude co-fluctuation events (moments of synchronized brain-wide activity) in all fish larvae using a rigorous statistical framework. Quantified the coactivation of brain regions by computing the root mean square (RMS) of pairwise node cofluctuations at each time point. Detected high-amplitude events, which significantly exceeded null RMS distributions obtained through circular permutations of regional time series (P < 0.05, false discovery rate adjusted at α = 0.05). Use bilateral regional time series in PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure4_rseries.npy and  PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure4_state_matrix.npy for analysis. Save key results in .npy files that may be used in subsequent analyses.
