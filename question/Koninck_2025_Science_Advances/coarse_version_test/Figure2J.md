Compute a set of comprehensive structural predictors from the directed and undirected SC matrices, as well as the inter-regional distances. Perform the PCA analysis of structural predictors. Visualize the relative and cumulative explained variance of the the first 10 PCs. Use SC_matrix_directed_terminals_expanded.npy, SC_matrix_undirected_terminals_expanded.npy, and PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure1_FC_matrix.npy for analysis.
