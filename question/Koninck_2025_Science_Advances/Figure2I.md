I want to evaluate how structural graph properties (predictors) explain the variance in functional connectivity (FC) relative to the structural connectivity (SC) network itself. Compute a set of comprehensive structural predictors from the directed and undirected SC matrices, as well as the inter-regional distances. Explore how each predictor contributes to the FC matrix after subtracting the average effect. Use SC_matrix_directed_terminals_expanded.npy, SC_matrix_undirected_terminals_expanded.npy, and PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure1_FC_matrix.npy for analysis. Brain region centroids are stored  in the atlas folder. Before analysis, use PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure1_excluded_regions.npy to identify regions to exclude from these SC matrices.
