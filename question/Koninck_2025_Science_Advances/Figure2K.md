I want to figure out whether structural properties collectively contribute to functional connectivity (FC) through the correlation analysis of the PC1 of structural predictors and the FC matrix. Compute a set of comprehensive structural predictors from the directed and undirected SC matrices, as well as the inter-regional distances. Perform the PCA analysis of structural predictors. Calculate the element-wise Pearson correlation between the half matrices (without the diagonal) of PC1 and FC. Visualize these half matrices in a combined square matrix after normalizing them respectively. Use SC_matrix_directed_terminals_expanded.npy, SC_matrix_undirected_terminals_expanded.npy, and PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure1_FC_matrix.npy for analysis. Brain region centroids are stored  in the atlas folder. Before analysis, use PROCESS_DATA_DIR/Figure1_excluded_regions.npy to identify regions to exclude from these SC matrices.
