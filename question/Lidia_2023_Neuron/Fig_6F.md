To determine if the C. elegans neuropeptide connectome exhibits a rich club organization, where high-degree hub neurons are more densely interconnected with each other than would be expected by chance, the rich-club coefficient was calculated based on the neuropeptide degree k of 302 neurons and compared against randomized null models that preserved the same degree sequence. The line plot displays the actual connectivity (black solid line), the random baseline (black dotted line), and the normalized coefficient (red solid line). The grey shaded area identifies the rich club regime where the normalized coefficient > 1, indicating that high-degree neurons are more densely interconnected than expected by chance. Error bars represent the standard deviation based on 1,000 random permutations.
