To identify distinct topological regimes, this analysis categorizes 92 neuropeptide-receptor (NPP(neuropeptide)-GPCR(G protein-coupled receptor)) signaling systems by mapping the discrete neuron counts expressing each ligand and receptor. The scatter plot shows the number of neurons expressing a particular GPCR (x-axis) versus the number expressing the corresponding NPP gene (y-axis) for each of 92 individual networks. Dashed lines at 50 neurons partition the plot into four quadrants. Filled circles indicate receptor expression; empty circles indicate neuropeptide expression.
