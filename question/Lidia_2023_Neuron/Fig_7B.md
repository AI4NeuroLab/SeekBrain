To identify subgroups of neurons with similar peptidergic connectivity patterns and characterize the mesoscale substructure of the neuropeptidergic network, dimensionality reduction methods were applied to the adjacency matrix of the mid-range aggregate network. Principal Component Analysis (PCA) was used to project the high-dimensional connectivity data into two dimensions . Neurons were color-coded by their identified functional cores: Motor core (green), Sensory core (purple), and Hubs core (yellow), with the remaining neurons classified as the Periphery (grey). Datapoint markers represent neuronal classifications (Pharynx, Sensory neuron, Interneuron, and Motorneuron).
