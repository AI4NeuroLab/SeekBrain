To quantitatively compare the peptidergic connectivity strength among the identified mesoscale clusters and verify if these functional cores represent distinct hierarchical tiers within the neuropeptide connectome, a violin plot was generated based on the classification of 302 neurons into the Motor core, Hubs core, Sensory core, and Periphery, showing the distribution of neuropeptide mid-range indegree for each group. Statistical significance between the clusters was assessed to determine if the Hubs core possesses a uniquely dominant topological position compared to the sensory and motor modules.
