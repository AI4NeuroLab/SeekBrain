To compare the mean area of high-activity subspace receptive fields between putative excitatory (regular-spiking) and inhibitory (narrow-spiking) neurons within local auditory cortex populations. Based on the high-activity SSRF regions (firing rate > 80% of maximum) for each neuron across 39 recording sites, the mean fraction of the shared 2D tuning subspace covered by the SSRFs was calculated separately for regular-spiking and narrow-spiking neuronal groups. A scatter plot was generated to compare these mean coverage fractions between the two cell type groups across recording sites. Finally, the p-value from a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test comparing the paired mean coverage fractions across the 39 sites is reported on the figure.
