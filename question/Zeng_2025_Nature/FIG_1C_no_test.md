To visualize the global transcriptomic organization of 568,654 high-quality single cells from the developing and adult mouse visual cortex across developmental stages from embryonic day 11.5 (E11.5) to postnatal day 56 (P56), project cells into a two-dimensional Uniform Manifold Approximation and Projection (UMAP) space and color cells by the 15 major cell classes to reveal class-level clustering structure and relationships among neuronal, progenitor, and non-neuronal populations.
