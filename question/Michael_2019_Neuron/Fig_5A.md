For 121 cerebellar neurons identified as efferent neurons whose axons project outside the cerebellum, including 91 eurydendroid cells (ECs) and 30 Purkinje cells (PCs) from the larval zebrafish brain at 6 dpf, pairwise morphological similarity scores computed using the NBLAST algorithm are subjected to unsupervised hierarchical clustering with Ward's method. PCs exclusively send their projections to the MON, whereas ECs provide a more diverse output of the cerebellum. And the resulting dendrogram is shown with colors indicating the eight major clusters, to reveal the morphological organization and classification of cerebellar output neurons.
