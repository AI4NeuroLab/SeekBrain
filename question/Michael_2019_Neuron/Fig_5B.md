To visualize the spatial distribution of cerebellar efferent neurons clustered by morphological similarity, all 121 cerebellar output cells (91 eurydendroid cells and 30 Purkinje cells) from the larval zebrafish brain at 6 dpf were clustered by hierarchical clustering based on NBLAST similarity scores (Figure 5A), then all neurons were mirrored to the right hemisphere and overlaid on the outline of the standard brain (gray surface), with different colors representing the different clusters from Figure 5A and Purkinje cells (cluster 7) omitted for clarity, and black rectangles indicating regions zoomed in for detailed viewing.
