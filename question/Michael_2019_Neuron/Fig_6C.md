To visualize the morphological diversity of neuronal clusters identified through NBLAST-based hierarchical clustering, 2,143 neurons were classified by morphology and innervation pattern using NBLAST and subjected to hierarchical clustering based on similarity scores; the dendrogram was cut to yield 22 distinct clusters, and individual clusters of neurons are displayed side by side in the semi-transparent outline of the standard brain (gray), with each neuron rendered as connected line segments following its SWC tree structure, and asterisks denoting clusters of known cell types including cluster 13 (oculomotor neurons) and cluster 14 (retinal ganglion cells, RGCs). Use the navis library for NBLAST.
