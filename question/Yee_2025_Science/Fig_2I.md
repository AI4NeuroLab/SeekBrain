Minimal D2 receptor–mediated inhibitory postsynaptic currents evoked by local stimulation near and far from the indirect pathway spiny projection neuron dendrite were compared. Normalized D2-IPSC amplitude, mean dendritic Δ[DA], and peak dendritic Δ[DA] evoked by stimulation from increasing distances from the dendrite were quantified together with the length constants (λ) of single exponential fits.
