Averaged G-Flamp2 ΔF/F0 measurements (2PSP) from dendrites of indirect pathway spiny projection neurons (iSPNs) in response to stimulation and block by sulpiride (0.5 μM), together with the summary of peak dendritic Δ[cAMP], were used to determine whether spatially restricted electrical stimulation reduced [cAMP] at dendrites of iSPNs and whether this effect depended upon D2 receptor (D2R) activation.
