To better understand the spatial pattern of this D2 receptor–dependent signaling, local changes of dopamine concentration (Δ[DA]) and voltage-gated Ca2+ channel (VGCC) modulation were measured at different locations on the same dendrite after dopamine release. Normalized Δ[Ca2+] amplitude evoked by dopamine release at dendritic regions of interest (ROIs) exposed to <1 μM, 1 to 10 μM, or >10 μM concentrations of dopamine was compared, and VGCC modulation at dendritic ROIs was summarized.
