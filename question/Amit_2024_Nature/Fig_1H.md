To determine whether ventromedial hypothalamus (VMHvl) line-attractor dynamics are intrinsic to this hypothalamic nucleus, the continuous two-dimensional (2D) latent factor trajectories from two-photon imaging were analyzed via recurrent switching linear dynamical systems (rSLDS) modeling to compute a dense 2D vector field, and the direction flow of time was visualized as flow fields overlaid with vector arrows on the neural trajectory in the state space.
