For 121 cerebellar efferent neurons (91 eurydendroid cells and 30 Purkinje cells) from the larval zebrafish brain at 6 dpf, pairwise morphological similarity scores computed using the NBLAST algorithm are subjected to unsupervised hierarchical clustering with Ward's method, and the resulting dendrogram is shown with colors indicating the eight major clusters, to reveal the morphological organization and classification of cerebellar output neurons.
