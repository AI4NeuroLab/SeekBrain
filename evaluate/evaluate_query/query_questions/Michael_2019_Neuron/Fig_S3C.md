To measure the accuracy of the registration, five landmarks within the SYP pattern (fiber landmarks) and 20 from compact cell clusters in various co-registered Gal4 patterns (nuclei landmarks), which were easily recognizable across individual brains, were identified and the Euclidean distances to their counterparts in the standard brain were measured; these distances are displayed as a boxplot with magenta indicating nuclei-based landmarks from Gal4 patterns and green indicating fiber-based landmarks from SYP patterns, to visualize the alignment accuracy for the individual fiducial points and enable comparison between landmark types.
