To investigate the relationship between inter-regional connection strength and anatomical distance in the larval zebrafish brain, the brain was divided into 36 non-overlapping regions covering ~82% of the brain volume, which were split into left and right hemispheres for a total of 72 brain regions; for each pair of brain regions, normalized connection strength (calculated by summing neurite lengths from all neurons that connect them and normalizing by the total volume of the two brain regions) was plotted against the Euclidean distance between their centers of mass, and the Pearson correlation coefficient was calculated and displayed on the plot.
