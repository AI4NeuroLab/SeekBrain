# Animal models in neurobiology research

Relate the research question to the model's relevant biology and experimental accessibility. Large identifiable neurons, genetic tractability, natural behavioral specializations, and similarity to human systems support different kinds of investigation.

For an existing dataset, establish what its species and preparation preserve: intact animal, explant, slice, culture, organoid, or human observation. Distinguish shared mechanisms from model-specific anatomy, physiology, and behavior when interpreting or extrapolating results. Consider alternative models when choosing a new experimental system is within the user's scope.
