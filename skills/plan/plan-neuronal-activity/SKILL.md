# Recording and manipulating neuronal activity

Identify the measured signal before choosing an analysis: extracellular spikes or field potentials, intracellular voltage or current, or optical activity indicators. Relate indicator kinetics, sampling, and recording coverage to the timing and population claims being tested. Calcium and other activity proxies have different relationships to firing and subthreshold activity.

Separate activity associated with a stimulus or behavior from intervention evidence. Inactivation and activation address different questions about necessity and sufficiency, subject to targeting, efficacy, timing, and the effects of the intervention on the surrounding circuit.

For connection mapping, distinguish anatomical projections from functional responses and direct from polysynaptic effects. Paired recording and targeted stimulation can test the direction and physiological properties of connections when the corresponding data are available.
