# Anatomical techniques

Match the anatomical claim to labeling specificity, spatial resolution, and reconstruction scope. Tissue organization, single-neuron morphology, subcellular organization, regional projections, and identified synapses are distinct measurement levels.

For projection tracing, establish tracer direction and which cells or processes are labeled. A regional projection is not sufficient evidence of a particular cell-to-cell synapse. Serial electron microscopy can resolve synaptic structure, whereas anatomical connectivity alone does not determine physiological sign, strength, or recruitment during behavior.

Choose comparisons that the available images and reconstructions can support; account for sectioning, registration, and incomplete labeling where they affect the biological inference.
