## Interpretation focus

Specify the level of abstraction and assumptions of the neuron or population model. Compare the reported quantities with the actual recordings. Check heterogeneity, noise, input statistics and time scales before translating model behavior into biological explanations.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images.
