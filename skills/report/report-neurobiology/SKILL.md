## Interpretation focus

Use the actual experiment to connect molecular, cellular and systems explanations. For visual cortical development, consider the definitions of cell identity, maturation, wiring and function separately. Describe the manipulation or measurement that would discriminate competing mechanisms.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images. Use textbook background to identify relevant concepts and boundaries, and retrieved studies to assess specific or recent explanations. Preserve uncertainty and methodological caveats.
