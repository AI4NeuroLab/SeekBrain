Author/editor: Bertil Hille.
Catalog scope: Classic reference on ion channels, membrane biophysics, gating, and conductance. Graduate–research.

## Interpretation focus

Distinguish expression, channel abundance, channel state, conductance and measured current. Relate channel-level observations to membrane and firing behavior only at the evidence level established by the experiment. Gene-expression differences alone do not establish electrophysiological changes.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images. Use textbook background to identify relevant concepts and boundaries, and retrieved studies to assess specific or recent explanations. Preserve uncertainty and methodological caveats. Keep skill guidance out of the report's experimental Results and cite the consulted scientific source rather than this skill file.
