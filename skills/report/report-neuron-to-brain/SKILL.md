# From Neuron to Brain

Author/editor: John G. Nicholls et al.; author team varies by edition.
Catalog scope: Develops neural signaling and circuit function from cellular physiology and experimental evidence. Undergraduate–graduate.

## Reading and source use

Source workbook: Neuroscience_Textbooks.xlsx, Textbooks sheet, row 11. The catalog supplies title, authors and scope; edition and chapter body are not supplied.

Choose chapters from the user's scientific question, the plan and the actual validated results. Look for this title and author in the supplied reference paths or use the source entry above when available. Existing read/ls/grep/find tools and read-only bash can inspect supported local materials; the configured literature search can retrieve relevant research papers. Use available original studies when a textbook chapter cannot be accessed, and describe their source type accurately. There is no fixed reading or search quota.

This file provides authored reading and reasoning guidance. It contains no textbook chapter excerpts and does not establish that the book has been read. Do not invent an edition, page number, quotation or book-supported claim. Record the actual source locator and reading scope when using material in the report. Treat publisher metadata and contents as metadata only.

## Interpretation focus

Connect measured physiology to the specific signaling process tested. Consider recording conditions, stimulus, cell type and synaptic versus intrinsic contributions. Describe what the assay directly measures before interpreting its consequences for circuits.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images. Use textbook background to identify relevant concepts and boundaries, and retrieved studies to assess specific or recent explanations. Preserve uncertainty and methodological caveats. Keep skill guidance out of the report's experimental Results and cite the consulted scientific source rather than this skill file.
