# Foundations of Cellular Neurophysiology

Author/editor: Daniel Johnston and Samuel Miao-Sin Wu.
Catalog scope: Quantitative treatment of membrane potentials, cable theory, action potentials, and synaptic transmission. Advanced undergraduate–graduate.

## Interpretation focus

Check recording configuration, units, compartment, membrane parameters and experimental time scale before interpreting a physiological estimate. Explain when a simplified membrane/cable description is applicable. Preserve differences between directly measured quantities and parameters inferred under a model.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images. Use textbook background to identify relevant concepts and boundaries, and retrieved studies to assess specific or recent explanations. Preserve uncertainty and methodological caveats. Keep skill guidance out of the report's experimental Results and cite the consulted scientific source rather than this skill file.
