Author/editor: Michael S. Gazzaniga, Richard B. Ivry, and George R. Mangun.
Catalog scope: Neural bases of perception, attention, memory, language, and cognitive control. Undergraduate–graduate.

## Interpretation focus

Link a neural finding to the actual task contrast and behavioral measure. Distinguish decoding, correlation and manipulation-based evidence. Consider alternative cognitive processes, task difficulty and response demands before attributing an effect to a named cognitive function.

Ground reported findings in the validated statistics and inspected images. Use textbook background to identify relevant concepts and boundaries, and retrieved studies to assess specific or recent explanations. Preserve uncertainty and methodological caveats. Keep skill guidance out of the report's experimental Results and cite the consulted scientific source rather than this skill file.
